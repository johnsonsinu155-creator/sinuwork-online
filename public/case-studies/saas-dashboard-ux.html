Overview

The dashboard was where the team's decisions actually got made — but the interface hadn't kept up with how those decisions were being made. Widgets buried key numbers, filters fought the workflow, and every sprint added more without resolving the friction underneath.

I redesigned the experience in step with the team's own sprint cadence: testing, shipping, and refining every two weeks rather than delivering one big reveal.
