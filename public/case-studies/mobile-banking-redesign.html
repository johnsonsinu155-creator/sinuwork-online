Overview

Everyday banking tasks — checking a balance, moving money, finding a statement — had accumulated friction over years of feature bolt-ons. The brief: rebuild the experience around how people actually use the app, without breaking what already worked.

I led the redesign end to end: research, flow mapping, wireframes through high-fidelity UI, and the component system that now carries it forward.

Process

Four moves, in order.

01 / Research

Listen first

Ran sessions with 8 participants, then synthesized findings into 3 personas covering distinct banking habits and trust levels.

02 / Diagnose

Map the friction

Heuristic evaluation and journey mapping across 5 core user flows surfaced exactly where and why people got stuck.

03 / Design

Wireframe to hi-fi

Iterated from low-fidelity structure to production-ready UI, cutting task steps by roughly a third along the way.

04 / Align

Bring the room along

Presented decisions to stakeholders, tying each design choice back to business KPIs and user goals.

System

A shared language for the team.

Every screen was rebuilt on a token-based component library — consistent spacing, states, and interaction patterns other designers and engineers could pick up without guessing.
